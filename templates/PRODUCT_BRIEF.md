## Standard admin pages (if applicable)
1. Sign-in, first-run secure admin setup, forgotten password, session activity.
2. Dashboard: today's work, recent changes, immediate next action.
3. My Settings: language, appearance, accessibility, notifications, profile.
4. Organization Admin: company/branches, users/roles, permissions by action/resource, audit, backups/recovery, import/export, subscription/licence, device/seat usage.
5. Customer-support consent: explicitly authorize temporary support, see activity, revoke.
6. Help, status, version and recovery; user-readable errors.
7. Domain pages selected from researched customer journey, not copied wholesale from other products.
