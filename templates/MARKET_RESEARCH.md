# Market research | first paid pilot (source-led)

Research date, country, audience, intended deployment, and price currency. Before defining screen inventory, study **at least five meaningful competitors/alternatives where available**. Never fabricate competitor capabilities, prices, download counts, market shares or laws.

## Customer discovery
Ask 5 real prospective buyers about: (1) last incident costing time/money, (2) current workaround, (3) number of staff/devices/sites, (4) must-have first successful result, (5) budget and trust/security objections. Record responses and approval owner; do not invent interview notes.

## Strategy
- Differentiation: cheaper / easier / offline / faster / region-specific / safer / better workflow (evidence required).
- Offer: basic core journey with bounded users/devices, support and clearly priced extras.
- Feasibility: device hardware, network, data import, API dependencies, offline, legal restrictions.
- Estimate monthly customer support + hosting + payment fees, onboarding cost, contribution margin and refund exposure.
- Prioritize using customer pain × willingness to pay × feasibility × data risk; **not** number of screens.
- Risks: false price, unpaid trial conversion, support overload, regional rules, integrations under customer control.
- Open assumptions: all UNKNOWN until sourced or owner-approved.

## Market gates
- At least five compared alternatives when obtainable, with real links and checked-on dates.
- Document what customers can accomplish on day 1 and how correctness is proved.
- Define the **one** paid customer workflow; defer optional AI/automation until trusted data foundation works.
- Approval: buyer persona, paid workflow, price hypothesis, must-have vs later and deployment mode.
