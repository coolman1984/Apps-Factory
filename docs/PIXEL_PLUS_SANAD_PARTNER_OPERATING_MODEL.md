# Pixel Plus × Sanad Business Advisory: partner operating plan
Owner direction: 2026-10-09 • **Concept approved, commercial/legal terms pending owner's Sunday 2026-10-11 briefing.**

## Identity and boundaries
Pixel Plus is the software/design/automation/licensing product company, with its own brand and customer contracts.
Sanad Business Advisory is a separate advisory partner with its own brand and operating accountability, working with customers to improve management, accounting preparation, planning, purchasing, operations and growth. The owner supplied photographs of each company Facebook presence; use only brand-authored logo files with permission for polished website material. Do not silently rebrand Pixel Plus as Sanad or represent an uncontracted partnership as a legal joint venture.

## Customer journey (small Egyptian businesses)
1. A customer sees authentic Pixel Plus program screenshots and interactive synthetic demo and installs a 14-day trial.
2. Pixel Plus uses a trusted licence workflow to activate the approved trial, then sells one core product and the chosen infrastructure tier.
3. In Guide, Owner Insights and product page, they see **Rafaa Radar (included)**: explainable business warnings and one safe scenario.
4. They may choose the optional **Rafaa Full** expansion licence (monthly or perpetual) for deeper finance, close, debts, tax guides, procurement, FP&A and cashflow analysis.
5. **Only if the customer asks for human help**, offer a separate Sanad consultation: onboarding, chart-of-accounts mapping, regular close review, purchasing planning, growth scenarios, staff process and tax liaison, under Sanad's scope and professional terms.
6. Roles: business owner approves, internal accountant sees posted entries and period close, manager sees authorized operations and scenarios, Sanad adviser sees **only explicitly shared views/timeboxed assignments**, never all records by default.
7. A single consented enquiry may lead to a quote with **clearly separate** product licence, add-on, support and advisory line items. A customer can cancel Sanad while continuing to own/use Pixel Plus's perpetual modules.

## Four separate revenue lines (no bundling trap)
A. Software: initial Store licence (trial/monthly/perpetual; launch pricing NOT decided).
B. Infrastructure: Solo/Connected/Mobile Operations/Cloud Business support and connectivity, only if capabilities proven.
C. Financial growth expansion: included Rafaa Radar vs optional paid Rafaa Full. Proposed exploratory pricing in dedicated research is **not public/approved**.
D. Human services: Sanad diagnostic session, one-time implementation support, monthly accounting/management coaching, operational review, growth/referral service. Services are NOT bundled into the add-on's perpetual licence by default.

A seller can explain benefits, but must not say software replaces qualified accountants/tax lawyers or guarantees growth/tax compliance. Any legal guide source must show official law, effective date, applicability, limitations and last qualified review.

## Consent, privacy, confidentiality and commercial operations
- Cross-company transfer of any name/contact/financial data is OFF by default. At most a self-requested lead with notice of recipient, purpose, retention and withdrawal, and confirmation from the customer.
- To provide advice, the client chooses a scoped report/export or grants a separate temporary read-only role restricted to named data, period, personnel, and specific adviser; audit every access and terminate on expiry/revocation. No shared static accounts and never direct database copies or admin access.
- Raw employee salaries, individual ID numbers, personal medical information, bank data and detailed client financial statements are not passed to marketing tools or messaging bots.
- Capture minimal opt-in pipeline status **lead → first contact → product demo → advisory diagnosis → quote → pilot → paid/closed**; differentiate software and adviser lead owners. No unsolicited Telegram/WhatsApp messages; Telegram owner approvals are operational security notices, not client marketing.
- Create written engagement letter outlining deliverables, whether advice is bookkeeping, management assistance, statutory tax representation or other regulated service, client approvals, deadlines, professional reviewers, warranties/limitations, complaint/refund route.
- Track commercial metrics: number of qualified leads, first-value time, guided demo completion, licensed conversions, paid Rafaa upgrades, opt-in Sanad consultations, renewals, hours saved, client-reported satisfaction, support and complaint volume. Never claim numeric savings without measurement.

## Initial offering experiments (NOT published prices)
1. **Software alone**: customer self-serves basic workflows; guides + demo + basic support.
2. **Business Readiness session**: customer sends agreed summary, Sanad and owner identify three ranked actions to prevent a near-term problem; one-off advisory quote.
3. **Monthly Growth Check**: Sanad reviews the customer's consented Rafaa snapshot, cash forecast, overdue receivables and supplier obligations, and agrees next actions; clear outputs and scheduled meeting.
4. **Growth/Expansion review**: scenario analysis (new staff/branch, pricing, stock purchases) with a documented plan and owner approval; don't generate guaranteed return forecasts.

Every experiment must show price/effort/actual human scope and have an opt-out; do not send customer data to advisers before consent.
